# Analytics, Operational Reporting & CSAT

## Operational Metrics
The HR Shared Services 2.0 module provides operational dashboards tracking key service delivery indicators:

1. **Volume & Inflow Rates**:
   - Tickets created by channel (Portal, Email, Teams, WhatsApp).
   - Inquiries by category (Payroll, Benefits, Leaves, Letters, Policy).
2. **Turnaround & SLA Compliance**:
   - First Response Time (FRT) vs. SLA benchmark.
   - Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR).
   - SLA Breach Percentage across queues and tiers.
   - Net Resolution Time excluding paused durations.
3. **Agent Productivity & Capacity**:
   - Active ticket load per specialist.
   - First Contact Resolution (FCR) rate.
   - Escalation frequencies per team.

## Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) Engine
Upon request resolution, employees receive a structured 5-star CSAT survey evaluating:
* **Overall Rating** (1-5 stars)
* **Timeliness** (1-5 stars)
* **Agent Knowledge** (1-5 stars)
* **Helpfulness & Empathy** (1-5 stars)
* **Satisfaction Rate Calculation**: Percentage of responses rated 4 or 5 stars (`satisfied` / `very_satisfied`).
