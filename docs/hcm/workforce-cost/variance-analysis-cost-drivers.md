# Variance Analysis & Cost Drivers

## Multi-Dimensional Comparison
Compares workforce expenditures across:
- `budget_vs_actual`
- `plan_vs_actual`
- `forecast_vs_actual`
- `prior_period_vs_actual`

## Driver Decomposition
Isolates the root economic drivers explaining cost variances:
- **Headcount Growth %**: Proportion of variance driven by net employee additions.
- **Salary / Rate %**: Proportion driven by compensation or market rate adjustments.
- **Overtime %**: Proportion driven by elevated unscheduled overtime hours.
- **Contractor %**: Proportion driven by contingent worker reliance.
