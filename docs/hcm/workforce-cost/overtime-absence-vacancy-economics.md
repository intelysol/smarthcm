# Overtime, Absence & Vacancy Economics

## Opportunity & Drag Quantification
1. **Overtime Economics**: Connects schedules, attendance punches, and payroll multiplier tiers to monitor premium labor spend.
2. **Absence Economics**: Ingests lost hours from Epic 2.48 (`hcm_absence_events`) and calculates direct paid absence plus replacement coverage drag.
3. **Vacancy Economics**: Quantifies the business drag of open unfulfilled positions based on duration of vacancy and budgeted role compensation.
