# Cost Classification & Cost Nature Framework

## Cost Categories
Every cost line is assigned a specific functional classification:
1. `direct_labor`: Regular wages, overtime, shift premiums, project-assigned labor.
2. `indirect_labor`: Administrative, supervisory, support, and internal non-billable time.
3. `burden`: Employer payroll taxes, social security, pension contributions, health premiums.
4. `acquisition`: Recruitment fees, agency costs, advertising, onboarding.
5. `development`: Training, certifications, conferences, tuition assistance.
6. `mobility`: Relocation stipends, expatriate allowances, per diems.
7. `contractor`: External agency staff, freelancer invoices, temporary contracts.
8. `opportunity`: Vacancy economic drag, unplanned absence loss.

## Cost Nature Partitioning
To maintain absolute financial integrity, cost figures are strictly tagged with their exact nature:
- `ACTUAL`: Verifiable transactional data from payroll runs, paid invoices, and clock punches.
- `PLANNED`: Budgeted figures from approved workforce plans.
- `FORECAST`: Statistical projections for upcoming periods.
- `ESTIMATED`: Standard-cost approximations.
- `ALLOCATED`: Cost lines redistributed across cost centers and projects.
- `SCENARIO`: Modeled what-if simulations.
