# Employee vs. Contractor Economics

## Comparative Cost Profiles
Evaluates full economic trade-offs:
- **Full-Time Employees**: Base salary + 25% burden (taxes, benefits, health insurance, leave entitlements, onboarding).
- **Independent Contractors**: Higher gross hourly rates without employer burden, benefits, or severance commitments.
- **Break-Even Analysis**: Identifies when ongoing contractor utilization exceeds the total burdened cost of permanent staffing.
