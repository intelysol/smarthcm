# Scenario Economic Modeling (What-Ifs)

## Supported Scenario Types
1. `hire`: Financial impact of net new positions.
2. `freeze`: Cost savings from delayed or canceled recruitment pipelines.
3. `contractor_substitution`: Conversion of contractor roles to direct employees.
4. `automate`: Technology investment vs. annual labor savings, computing ROI % and payback in months.
5. `relocate` / `reskill`: Labor cost adjustments from regional mobility or skill upgrades.
