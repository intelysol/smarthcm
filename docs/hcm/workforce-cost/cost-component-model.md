# Normalized Cost Component Model

## Standard Component Types
The domain normalizes disparate upstream cost types into standard enterprise components:
- `BASE_PAY`: Fixed salary or contracted hourly earnings.
- `OVERTIME`: Premium pay for hours exceeding statutory or scheduled thresholds.
- `PREMIUM_PAY`: Night shift, weekend, or hazard allowances.
- `BONUS`: Performance bonuses, thirteenth-month pay.
- `INCENTIVE`: Sales commissions, piece-rate incentives.
- `ALLOWANCE`: Housing, transport, or meal stipends.
- `EMPLOYER_TAX`: Statutory payroll taxes paid by the employer.
- `EMPLOYER_CONTRIBUTION`: Pension, retirement fund, or life insurance match.
- `BENEFITS`: Employer-sponsored medical, dental, or wellness plans.
- `EXPENSE`: Travel, lodging, and client entertainment reimbursements.
- `CONTRACTOR`: Invoiced third-party labor.
- `ABSENCE`: Direct paid sick leave or absence costs.
- `VACANCY`: Value of unfilled capacity in open positions.
