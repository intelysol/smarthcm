# Preboarding & First-Day Readiness

## Preboarding Phase
Preboarding spans the period between offer acceptance and the employee's official start date:
- Due date offsets are negative numbers (e.g. -3 days, -1 day).
- Primary milestones:
  - Document collection (Identity, Proof of Address, Degree certificates).
  - Digital form completion (Direct Deposit, Emergency Contacts).
  - Policy review & e-acknowledgements.
  - IT account creation and laptop hardware provisioning.

## First-Day Readiness KPI
A case is deemed **First-Day Ready** when all required preboarding tasks are 100% completed prior to or on the designated `start_date`.
