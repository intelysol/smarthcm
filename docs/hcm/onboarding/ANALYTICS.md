# Onboarding Analytics & Readiness Reporting

## KPIs
- **Active Onboarding Cases**: Cases in preboarding or in-progress.
- **Completed Cases**: Cases with 100% required tasks satisfied.
- **Completion Rate**: Percentage of started onboarding cases that successfully finished.
- **First-Day Readiness %**: Proportion of new hires whose preboarding checklist was 100% complete on or before day 1.
- **Overdue & Blocked Tasks**: Action items needing manager or HR intervention.
- **Average Duration**: Mean elapsed days from case creation to final milestone completion.
