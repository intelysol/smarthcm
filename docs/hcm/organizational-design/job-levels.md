# Job Levels & Career Tracks

## Career Tracks
Career tracks define distinct paths of professional contribution:
* **Individual Contributor (`individual_contributor`)**: Deepening craft and technical mastery without direct people management.
* **Management (`management`)**: People leadership, team operations, coaching, and resource allocation.
* **Technical Specialist / Fellow (`technical_specialist`)**: Elite domain expertise, enterprise architectural guidance, and innovation.
* **Executive (`executive`)**: Organizational strategy, fiduciary governance, and enterprise leadership.

---

## Career Levels vs. Job Levels
* **Career Levels (`career_levels`)**: Graded seniority ranks within a track (e.g. L1 Associate, L2 Professional, L3 Senior, L4 Staff, L5 Principal).
* **Job Levels (`job_levels`)**: Broad organizational level classification (e.g., Entry, Professional, Senior, Lead, Manager, Director, Executive).
* **Decoupled from Salary Grades**: Career levels define capability expectations and scope of impact, remaining independent from financial compensation grades (`job_grades`).
