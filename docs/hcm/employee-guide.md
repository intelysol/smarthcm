# Employee Self-Service Guide

The **Employee Self-Service Portal** gives every employee direct access to their personal HR data, daily clock-in tools, leave requests, and privacy management.

---

## Features & Navigation

### 1. Home Dashboard (`/portal`)
- Fast daily punch clock (Clock-In / Clock-Out) with timestamp logging.
- Overview of current leave balances (e.g., Annual, Sick).
- Notifications of company announcements and pending tasks.

### 2. My Requests (`/portal/requests`)
- Submit leave requests with reason and dates.
- Track real-time status: `Pending Approval`, `Approved`, or `Rejected`.

### 3. My Payslips (`/portal/pay`)
- Download monthly payslips as PDF.
- Review earnings breakdown, tax deductions, and employer contributions.

### 4. My Documents (`/portal/documents`)
- Upload identification, degree certificates, and signed agreements.
- Download verified company letters and tax forms.

### 5. Privacy & Data Rights (`/portal/privacy`)
- Exercise GDPR / Data Protection rights.
- Request data export, rectification, or account information deletion.
