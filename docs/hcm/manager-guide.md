# People Manager Guide

The **People Manager** workspace is optimized for team supervisors and department leads to oversee direct reports and streamline administrative tasks.

---

## Manager Workbench (`/manager/workbench`)

The Manager Workbench surfaces high-priority operational items without administrative clutter:

### 1. Team Overview & Roster
- View direct and indirect reports, job titles, and contact information.
- Inspect team hierarchy and reporting lines.

### 2. Pending Approvals (`/portal/manager/approvals`)
- **Leave Requests**: One-click approval or rejection with reason tracking.
- **Expense Claims**: Review receipts and approve employee expense reports.
- **Attendance Regularization**: Review clock-in miss explanations.

### 3. Team Attendance & Shifts
- Monitor team members' daily clock-in/out times.
- View upcoming team leaves on the shared team calendar to avoid scheduling conflicts.

### 4. Performance & Goals (`/manager/performance`)
- Set quarterly objectives and key results (OKRs) for reports.
- Conduct performance appraisals and submit review ratings.
