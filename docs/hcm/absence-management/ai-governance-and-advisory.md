# AI Governance, Ethics & Privacy Protection

## Ethical Guardrails
- **Strictly Advisory**: All AI insights are labeled `is_advisory_only: true`.
- **No Autonomous Discipline**: AI cannot issue warnings, dismissals, or punitive measures.
- **Privacy Preservation**: Medical diagnostic codes, prescription data, and clinical notes are cryptographically shielded and never ingested by AI models.
- **Fairness & Non-Bias**: Algorithms evaluate operational volume and patterns without profiling demographic or protected characteristics.
