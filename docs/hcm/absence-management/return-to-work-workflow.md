# Return-to-Work (RTW) Workflow

## Purpose & Scope
The Return-to-Work engine provides a structured transition for employees recovering from illness, surgery, or workplace injury before resuming full duties.

## Lifecycle Phases
```text
[Created] ──> [Active] ──> [Under Review] ──> [Completed]
                                 │
                                 └──> [Amended]
```
1. **Initiation**: Triggered automatically upon extended absence (> 5 days) or created by HR.
2. **Clearance & Verification**: Medical clearance recorded with fit-to-work status.
3. **Phased Ramp**: Capacity ramp-up over defined weekly stages.
4. **Completion**: Employee returns to 100% standard operational capacity.
