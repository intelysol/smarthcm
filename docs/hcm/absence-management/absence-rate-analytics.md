# Absence Rate & Pattern Analytics

## Enterprise Metrics
The analytics engine computes standard absence KPIs:
1. **Lost Time Rate**: Total hours absent divided by total contracted working hours.
2. **Frequency Rate**: Number of discrete absence spells per 100 employees.
3. **Bradford Factor**: Measures the operational disruptiveness of frequent short-term absences:
   $$\text{Bradford Factor} = S^2 \times D$$
   where $S$ is the number of absence spells in a 52-week period, and $D$ is the total number of absence days.

## Trend & Day-of-Week Analysis
Identifies systematic patterns (e.g., higher unplanned absence rates on Mondays or Fridays) across business units and departments.
