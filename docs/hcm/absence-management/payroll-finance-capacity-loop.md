# Payroll, Finance & Capacity Feedback Loop

## Closed-Loop Integration
1. **Payroll**: Unpaid absence periods and resolved reconciliation records feed directly into payroll adjustments for deduction calculation.
2. **Workforce Capacity (Epic 2.45)**: Real-time and forecasted absence rates reduce available operational capacity hours in capacity models.
3. **Scheduling (Epic 2.46)**: RTW capacity percentages (50%, 75%) dynamically constrain shift scheduling algorithms.
4. **Finance**: Overtime and temp-agency replacement expenditures triggered by unplanned absence are aggregated for labor cost variance reporting.
