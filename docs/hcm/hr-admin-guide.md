# HR Administrator Guide

The **HR Administrator** commands the workforce management domain within an organization. This includes employee master data, leave approvals, attendance tracking, payroll calculation, benefits administration, and document management.

---

## Key Functional Modules

### 1. Workforce Directory (`/portal/directory`)
- View and search all employees across departments.
- Inspect employee profiles, emergency contacts, job contracts, and organizational lines.

### 2. Time & Attendance Management (`/hcm/attendance/dashboard`)
- Real-time attendance dashboard: who is present, absent, on leave, or late.
- Shift scheduling, holiday calendar management, and biometric sync monitoring.
- Manual attendance regularization approvals.

### 3. Leave & Absence Management (`/portal/requests`)
- Company leave policies (Annual, Sick, Casual, Parental).
- Approval workflows with automated escalation.
- Accrual calculations and leave balance ledgers.

### 4. Payroll & Compensation (`/payroll/dashboard`)
- Salary components: base pay, allowances, overtime multipliers, tax deductions.
- Monthly payroll runs, automated payslip generation, and bank file exports.

### 5. Document Management (`/employee-documents/admin/templates`)
- Standardized templates for offer letters, NDAs, and performance agreements.
- E-signature tracking and audit logging.
