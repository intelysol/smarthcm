# Workforce Scenarios & Comparison Engine

## Scenario Types

1. **Base Plan**: The sanctioned baseline approved by leadership.
2. **Aggressive Growth**: Expands headcount (+15%) and recruitment velocity (+20%) to capture market opportunity.
3. **Cost Reduction**: Models headcount contraction (-12%) and selective position eliminations to conserve capital.
4. **Hiring Freeze**: Pauses non-critical external requisitions over specific months.
5. **Restructuring**: Reallocates positions and departments during mergers, acquisitions, or organizational realignments.

## Comparison Metrics

The comparison engine computes side-by-side matrices:
- Projected Closing Headcount
- Total Projected Hires & Exits
- Total Projected Labor Cost
- Absolute & Percentage Variance vs Base Plan
