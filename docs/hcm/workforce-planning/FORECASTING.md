# Workforce & Labor Cost Forecasting

## Full-Year Forecasting Logic

$$\text{Full-Year Forecast} = \text{Actual YTD} + \text{Remaining Forecast Period}$$

Forecasting avoids brittle, opaque ML models as mandatory runtime dependencies. It combines:
1. Actual payroll ledger expenditure and headcount through current period.
2. Planned additions, planned attrition, and approved compensation increments for the remainder of the fiscal year.
3. Variance tracking against approved budget cap.
