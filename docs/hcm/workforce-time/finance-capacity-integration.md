# Finance & Workforce Capacity Closed Loop

## 1. Closed-Loop Feedback
```text
Capacity Plan (Epic 2.45)
       ↓
Published Schedule (Epic 2.46)
       ↓
Actual Clock Events (Epic 2.47)
       ↓
Productive & Chargeable Hours
       ↓
Capacity Analytics & Utilization (Epic 2.45)
```

## 2. Finance Project Ledger
Chargeable time allocations (`hcm_time_allocations`) provide client and cost-center allocations to Finance for client invoicing and project cost accounting.
