# Learning Security & Privacy Standards

## Privacy & Surveillance Policy
- **Anti-Surveillance Protection**: Flow HCM LMS strictly prohibits continuous keystroke logging, webcam/screen surveillance, or micro-behavioral attention tracking during e-learning.
- Only verifiable progress milestones (module completion, quiz submission, session attendance) are captured for compliance audits.

## Scope & Access Control
- **Employee Self-Service (ESS)**: Employees access exclusively their own learning records, certificates, assessments, and IDPs.
- **Manager Self-Service (MSS)**: Managers view training progress, nominations, and compliance strictly for direct and indirect reports within their reporting hierarchy.
- **Tenant Isolation**: Cross-tenant data leaks are physically blocked by database tenancy keys and policy scopes.
