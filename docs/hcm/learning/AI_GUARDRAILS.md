# AI Guardrails & Non-Autonomous Standards

## Permitted AI Operations
- Recommending courses based on explicit skill gaps and declared career aspirations.
- Summarizing extensive learning transcripts into competency development highlights.
- Assisting course creators in authoring draft quiz questions and learning objectives.
- Explaining organizational prerequisite paths.

## Prohibited Operations (Strict Platform Violations)
- Autonomously denying an employee enrollment or training request.
- Ranking employees negatively or computing "resignation probability" based on training behavior.
- Generating automated disciplinary actions for overdue compliance.
- Making autonomous promotion, demotion, or compensation determinations.

All AI recommendations flag `is_advisory: true` and undergo guardrail audit verification.
