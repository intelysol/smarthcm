# Learning Analytics & ROI

## Key Learning Metrics

- **Completion Rate**: $\frac{\text{Completed Enrollments}}{\text{Total Started Enrollments}} \times 100$
- **Mandatory Compliance Rate**: $\frac{\text{Compliant Active Employees}}{\text{Total Assigned Employees}} \times 100$
- **Average Training Hours**: $\frac{\text{Total Hours Completed}}{\text{Active Headcount}}$
- **Assessment Pass Rate**: $\frac{\text{Passed Attempts}}{\text{Total Evaluated Attempts}} \times 100$

## Learning ROI & Evidence Principle

> **Critical Standard**: The platform clearly distinguishes **Observed Correlation** from **Causal Evidence**. Training completions and subsequent promotions or performance increases are reported as observable correlations unless controlled experimental methodology has been conducted.
