# HR Operations Cockpit & Workspace

## 1. Overview
The HR Operations Cockpit provides real-time cross-domain visibility into workforce administration health, lifecycle tasks, SLA adherence, and operational bottlenecks.

## 2. Dashboard KPI Domains
1. **Workforce Administration**:
   - Total & Active Headcount
   - Employees Joining / Leaving within 30 days
   - Incomplete Employee Master Records
   - Employees on Leave
2. **Lifecycle Transitions**:
   - Pending Personnel Action Requests (Transfers, Promotions, Job Changes)
   - Onboarding & Offboarding workflows
3. **Compliance & Requirements**:
   - Expiring Visas & Work Permits (< 30/60/90 days)
   - Expiring Professional Licenses
   - Missing Compliance Documents
4. **Documents & Records**:
   - Missing Required Documents
   - Documents Pending Verification
5. **Payroll & Benefits Operations**:
   - Pending Payroll-impacting Changes
   - Unprocessed Employee Salary Changes
   - Open Enrollment & Life Event Exceptions
6. **Integration & Data Quality**:
   - System Integrations Health Status & Retry Queues
   - Open Data Quality Anomalies
   - SLA Escalation Breaches
