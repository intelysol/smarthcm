# AI Advisory Guardrails & Features

## 1. Advisory-Only Principle
Artificial Intelligence features in HCM Workforce Administration are strictly advisory and informative.

### Mandatory Operational Guardrails:
- **Zero Autonomous Execution**: AI must NEVER approve, reject, or execute bulk changes autonomously.
- **Zero Record Mutation**: AI must NEVER modify employee master data, salaries, bank accounts, or organization structures.
- **Zero Governance Bypass**: AI recommendations must never bypass Separation of Duties or RBAC permissions.

## 2. Capabilities
- **Operational Queue Summaries**: Natural language summaries of queue bottlenecks and pending backlog.
- **Exception Root-Cause Explanations**: Diagnosing root causes for reconciliation mismatches or payroll synchronization errors.
- **Data Quality Anomaly Insights**: Explaining data anomalies and recommending remediation paths for HR specialists.
- **Checklist Generation Assistance**: Suggesting tailored checklist items based on employee role and transition type.
