# HR Governance & Operational Rules

## 1. Scope & Policies
Workforce Administration enforces tenant-specific operational governance rules without duplicating individual domain policy engines:
- **Effective-Date Boundaries**: Restricting changes too far in the past or future.
- **Backdated Change Controls**: Requiring explicit justification, dual authorization, and impact analysis for retro-effective changes.
- **Separation of Duties (SoD)**: Enforcing that the initiator of a bulk workforce change cannot be its sole approver.
- **Sensitive Field Guardrails**: Protecting compensation, tax identity, and disciplinary records during operational triage.

## 2. Configuration Health Diagnostics
The platform regularly inspects HCM configuration readiness:
- Workflow route existence and active approval stages.
- Base salary and pay grade structure integrity.
- Open enrollment and life-event calendar windows.
- Integration gateway endpoints and encryption keys.
