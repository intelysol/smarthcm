# Anti-Surveillance, Privacy & Contextual Fairness

## 1. Zero Surveillance Telemetry
The SmartHCM platform categorically bans employee monitoring and surveillance features:
- No keystroke counting or speed logging.
- No screen captures, webcam snapshots, or active application tracking.
- No mouse movement, idle wiggle, or covert background sensors.
- Productivity metrics are strictly derived from verified business outputs (tickets, completed orders, manufactured batches).

## 2. Differential Privacy & Small Group Suppression
- Any departmental, team, or positional cohort with fewer than 3 employees automatically suppresses individual metrics in reporting.
- Aggregation is forced to the parent organizational tier (e.g., Business Unit or Division).

## 3. Contextual Fairness
- Raw metrics are normalized against role type, shift conditions, tool availability, and onboarding tenure.
- Non-punitive diagnostics attribute bottlenecks to workflow design, handoff delays, or equipment bottlenecks rather than personal employee capability.
