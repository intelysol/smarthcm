# Cost-Productivity Integration (Epic 2.49)

## 1. Interaction with Workforce Cost

The Workforce Productivity domain directly consumes cost lines and macro-economic summaries generated by Epic 2.49 (`WorkforceCost`):
- `HcmWorkforceCostSnapshot`
- `HcmWorkforceCostLine`
- `HcmWorkforceCostEconomics`

## 2. Key Economic Metrics
- **Labor Cost per Unit**: Total workforce labor cost divided by total completed units.
- **Output per Workforce Dollar**: Units produced per dollar invested in total workforce compensation.
- **Cost Divergence Analysis**: Explains when productivity rates and labor costs move in opposite directions (e.g., due to overtime premiums or contractor substitution).
