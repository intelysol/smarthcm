# Schedule Exceptions & Operational Deviations

## Exception Model

When deviations from planned schedules occur, they are recorded in `hcm_schedule_exceptions`:
- `exception_type`:
  - `no_show`: Scheduled worker did not report to work.
  - `late_arrival`: Worker clocked in after grace threshold.
  - `missing_staff`: Critical role or minimum staffing count violated.
  - `skill_shortage`: Qualified specialist missing from shift.
  - `undercoverage`: Headcount below required coverage.
  - `overcoverage`: Excess workers rostered.
  - `excessive_overtime`: Actual hours exceed fatigue limit.
  - `leave_conflict`: Leave approved after shift was scheduled.

## Exception Lifecycle
1. `detected`: Automatically generated by `RealTimeCoverageService` or `ScheduleValidationService`.
2. `acknowledged`: Operations supervisor notified.
3. `assigned`: Assigned to dispatcher or HR specialist for resolution.
4. `resolved`: Root cause addressed (replacement assigned or leave recorded).
5. `closed`: Verified and archived.
