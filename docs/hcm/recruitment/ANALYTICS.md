# Recruitment Analytics & Funnel Reporting

## KPIs
- **Open Requisitions**: Total active openings approved for hire.
- **Active Applications**: Candidates currently moving through screening, interview, or offer stages.
- **Time to Hire**: Mean days from application submission to formal hire sanction.
- **Pending Offers**: Offers awaiting candidate acceptance.

## Funnel Yield Stages
Calculates conversion percentage across pipeline gates:
1. `Applications -> Screened`
2. `Screened -> Interviewed`
3. `Interviewed -> Offered`
4. `Offered -> Hired (Acceptance Rate)`
5. `Overall Yield (Hired / Total Applications)`
