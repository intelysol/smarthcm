# Cross-Domain Platform Integrations

## Upstream & Downstream Touchpoints
- **Workforce Planning (Epic 2.24)**: Consumes approved hiring requirements (`hiring_plan_id`) and positions (`position_plan_id`) to seed requisitions.
- **Core HR (Epic 2.1 & 2.2)**: Reads department & position definitions; hands off candidate on hire to create authoritative Employee master.
- **Onboarding (Epic 2.3)**: Notified upon hire decision to generate pre-joining tasks and orientation checklists.
- **Documents & Storage**: Stores resume and portfolio files with secure storage paths.
- **Analytics & Reporting (Epic 2.23)**: Exposes recruitment funnel and KPI aggregations.
