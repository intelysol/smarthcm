## 2. Content Quality Standards
Every public page adheres to the enterprise content standards:
1. **Concrete Product Language**: Replaces buzzwords ("revolutionary AI magic") with verifiable product descriptions ("deterministic rules engine", "geofenced coordinate validation").
2. **Operational Workflow Transparency**: Every feature and solution page includes a numbered end-to-end workflow demonstrating how the system operates from input to outcome.
3. **Internal Link Graphs**: Every module cross-links to its natural upstream and downstream operational dependencies (e.g. Attendance links to Scheduling, Leaves, and Payroll).

---

## 3. Industry Content Model
Each industry vertical page (`/industries/{slug}`) follows a standardized, in-depth evaluation:
1. **Core HR Challenges**: Statutory regulations and turnover rates specific to that vertical.
2. **Operational Workforce Friction**: Ground-level shift and safety issues (e.g. nurse fatigue limits, construction trade certifications).
3. **SmartHCM Capabilities**: Specific system features configured to mitigate these friction points.
4. **Supported Workflows & Data Involved**: Exact data entities tracked (licenses, cost codes, geofence coordinates).
5. **Role-Specific Benefits**: Concrete value delivered to front-line workers via ESS and operational leaders via WFM.
