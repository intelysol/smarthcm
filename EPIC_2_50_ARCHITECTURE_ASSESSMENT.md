## HCM Workforce Productivity, Performance-to-Cost, Labor Efficiency & Workforce ROI Intelligence

**Date:** September 2026  
**Status:** Approved & Ready for Implementation  
**Module:** `app/Domains/WorkforceProductivity/`

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

Epic 2.50 establishes the unified **Workforce Productivity, Labor Efficiency, Performance-to-Cost, and Workforce ROI Intelligence** layer for the SmartHCM enterprise platform.

It transforms raw workforce operational data into actionable productivity and economic intelligence without duplicating existing foundational engines. It answers:
1. **Workforce Productivity**: How much operational output (units, cases, tickets, revenue) is being generated per labor hour and per productive hour?
2. **Labor Utilization**: What proportion of attended or scheduled capacity is actively productive, and where are non-productive hours lost (waiting, meetings, admin, training)?
3. **Labor Efficiency**: How do productive hours compare to paid hours and scheduled hours? What is the output per FTE?
4. **Performance-to-Cost (Epic 2.49 Integration)**: What is the direct labor cost per unit of output and output generated per workforce dollar?
5. **Overtime & Absence Drag**: How does overtime affect hourly output rates, and what capacity and cost drag does unplanned absence cause?
6. **Turnover & Ramp-up Economics**: What is the productivity loss during vacancy and the ramp-up curve of new hires?
7. **Workforce Investment ROI**: What measurable financial and operational returns are generated by workforce investments (training, hiring, automation, process redesign)?
8. **Scenario Modeling**: How do proposed headcount, shift, or technology changes impact future output, labor costs, and workforce ROI?
9. **Ethical AI & Non-Surveillance**: Provide advisory, non-punitive bottleneck insights and optimization suggestions with zero employee surveillance or automated disciplinary scoring.

## 2. Critical Ownership Boundaries (Zero Duplicate Engines)

The architecture strictly respects the bounded contexts of existing platform domains:

| Domain | Authoritative Ownership | Workforce Productivity Interaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Workforce Capacity** (`app/Domains/WorkforcePlanning/` & Epic 2.45) | Owns capacity models, required capacity, available capacity, workload models, and capacity gaps. | **Consumes** required vs available capacity hours to calculate capacity utilization and capacity-to-output ratios. |
| **Workforce Scheduling** (`app/Domains/Attendance/` & Epic 2.46) | Owns shifts, rosters, planned schedules, planned hours, shift patterns, and coverage requirements. | **Consumes** planned hours and schedules to calculate schedule adherence, coverage, and effectiveness. |
| **Time & Attendance** (`app/Domains/Attendance/` & Epic 2.47) | Owns actual attendance, raw punches, worked hours, multi-tier overtime (`hcm_overtime_tier_records`), timesheets, and task allocations (`hcm_time_allocations`). | **Consumes** actual punch hours, worked minutes, and overtime records as authoritative labor time input. |
| **Workforce Absence** (`app/Domains/Absence/` & Epic 2.48) | Owns absence events, continuous periods, return-to-work phased plans, and operational impact (`hcm_absence_operational_impacts`). | **Consumes** lost capacity hours, substitution hours, and absence impact records to evaluate lost productivity drag. |
| **Workforce Cost** (`app/Domains/WorkforceCost/` & Epic 2.49) | Owns total workforce cost, labor cost lines, cost allocation, cost variance, and workforce economics (`hcm_workforce_cost_economics`). | **Consumes** cost lines, cost per hour, and total workforce cost to compute Cost per Output and Output per Workforce Dollar. |
| **Performance Management** (`app/Domains/Performance/`) | Owns goals, OKRs, competencies, appraisals, calibration records, and review ratings. | **Correlates** aggregate operational productivity with performance ratings where authorized; **never** replaces performance ratings or uses ratings as raw productivity scores. |
| **Learning Management** (`app/Domains/Learning/`) | Owns courses, training sessions, attendance, development plans, and training costs (`LearningTrainingCost`). | **Consumes** training hours and costs to evaluate post-training productivity changes and calculate Training ROI. |
| **Recruitment** (`app/Domains/Recruitment/`) | Owns job requisitions, candidate offers, hiring decisions, and onboarding milestones. | **Consumes** time-to-hire, hiring costs, and ramp-up duration to calculate Recruitment ROI. |
| **Finance** (`app/Domains/Finance/`) | Owns Chart of Accounts, General Ledger, accounting periods, financial actuals, revenue, and profit. | **Consumes** approved financial actuals and revenue figures for economic productivity and ROI calculations. Finance remains authoritative. |

## 3. Core Conceptual Flow

```text
ATTENDANCE (Punches / Timesheets)
    ↓
TIME (Worked Hours vs Idle / Break)
    ↓
CAPACITY (Available vs Required Hours)
    ↓
PRODUCTIVE TIME (Active Value-Add Hours)
    ↓
OUTPUT (Completed Cases, Units, Revenue)
    ↓
PRODUCTIVITY (Output / Productive Labor Input)
    ↓
LABOR COST (Workforce Cost per Unit of Output)
    ↓
EFFICIENCY (Productive vs Paid Hours, Output / FTE)
    ↓
WORKFORCE ROI ((Net Benefit - Investment Cost) / Cost)
    ↓
EXECUTIVE & OPERATIONAL INTELLIGENCE
```

### Critical Axioms:
- `Attendance ≠ Productivity`: Being present does not equal generating output.
- `Hours Worked ≠ Productive Hours`: Non-productive time (waiting for parts, meetings, admin, downtime) must be categorized.
- `Performance Rating ≠ Productivity Score`: Subjective manager appraisals and objective operational volume are distinct concepts.

## 4. Anti-Surveillance, Privacy & Non-Punitive Fairness

### 4.1 Anti-Surveillance Policy
This domain is explicitly engineered as a business intelligence tool, NOT an employee monitoring or surveillance tool.
- Prohibited: Keystroke monitoring, webcam capture, screen recording, mouse movement tracking, covert telemetry.
- Productivity must always be measured through **legitimate business output** (cases resolved, units produced, revenue billed, transactions completed).

### 4.2 Differential Privacy & Small Group Suppression
To prevent deducing individual employee output in small departments or teams:
- Minimum threshold: If a reporting group has fewer than 3 employees, individual metrics are suppressed or aggregated at the next organizational level.

### 4.3 Contextual Fairness
Productivity metrics account for contextual variations:
- Role type differences (e.g. software engineer vs call center representative).
- Shift differences (night shifts vs day shifts).
- Tool availability, client complexity, and tenure ramp-up.

## 6. Mathematical Formulations & Zero-Denominator Safety

### 6.1 Productivity Rate
$$\text{Productivity} = \frac{\text{Output Volume}}{\text{Productive Labor Hours}}$$
*Safety Rule*: If $\text{Productive Labor Hours} \le 0$ or $\text{Output Volume} \text{ is null}$, result is strictly `null` (`N/A`), NEVER `0` or division-by-zero exception.

### 6.2 Utilization Rate
$$\text{Utilization} = \frac{\text{Productive Hours}}{\text{Available Capacity Hours}} \times 100\%$$

### 6.3 Labor Cost per Unit
$$\text{Cost per Unit} = \frac{\text{Direct Labor Cost}}{\text{Completed Units}}$$

### 6.4 Output per Workforce Dollar
$$\text{Output per Dollar} = \frac{\text{Completed Units}}{\text{Total Workforce Cost}}$$

### 6.5 Workforce Investment ROI
$$\text{ROI} = \frac{\text{Net Benefit} - \text{Investment Cost}}{\text{Investment Cost}} \times 100\%$$
Label must state: `CORRELATION` (statistical association) vs `CAUSAL` (experimental/controlled study).
